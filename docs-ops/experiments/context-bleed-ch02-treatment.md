## Distinctions

- `consciousness-vs-form-dependence` - Form depends on consciousness; consciousness does not depend on form.
- `identity-vs-form-stability` - Personal continuity and stability belong to identity integration, not permanent form.
- `inner-senses-vs-physical-senses` - Inner senses open freer perception, while physical senses constrain experience into three-dimensional form.
- `camouflage-vs-uncamouflaged-perception` - Physical reality is one camouflage system among many, contrasted with less-camouflaged perception.
- `time-sequence-vs-moment-point` - Physical time-order differs from intensified, associative, simultaneous psychological experience.
- `objective-reality-vs-consciousness-created-reality` - Rejects independently existing object-world in favor of reality as consciousness-made.
- `outer-ego-awareness-vs-inner-awareness` - The egotistical self ignores capacities and knowledge already active in larger consciousness.

## Mechanisms

- `psychic-to-physical-imprint` - Psychic and psychological manipulations are impressed onto the physical mold, producing body and environment.
- `dream-environment-manipulation` - Dream-state manipulation can rehearse or precipitate changes that later appear in physical reality.
- `form-follows-thought` - Changes in thought alter form, body, and environment continuously, whether consciously noticed or not.
- `multi-form-consciousness` - Consciousness can project, occupy, or generate multiple forms at once, including dream and thought forms.
- `thermal-electromagnetic-communication` - Nonphysical communication occurs through dense image-sequences carried by intensity rather than verbal symbols.
- `perception-by-psychological-structure` - Environment appears as it does because perceptive patterns are set by psychological structure and assumptions.
- `teacher-adaptation-across-systems` - Communication and teaching are reshaped to fit the perceptual rules and assumptions of each reality-system.
