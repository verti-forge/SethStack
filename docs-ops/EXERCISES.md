# Exercises

Append-only catalog of reader-directed exercises named in the source text.

Capture only. Do not log results here. Predictions are fixed at capture time.

### EX-01 — Dream-change suggestion
Source: SS Ch.2 (around the dream-environment-manipulation passage)
Setup: Before sleep, suggest a specific dream in which a desired change is seen and let the dream environment carry it.
Predicted class of experience: Dream imagery or dream-environment change that may later correspond to a physical-life shift under certain conditions.
Chair-friendly: yes

### EX-02 — Inner-senses stage exercise
Source: SS Ch.4 (inner-senses stage exercise passage)
Setup: Sit quietly, close your eyes, imagine the room-stage gone dark, and receptively listen and look with the inner senses for a few moments before sleep or at rest.
Predicted class of experience: Snatches of inner sights or sounds and a new focus in another dimension of awareness.
Chair-friendly: yes
